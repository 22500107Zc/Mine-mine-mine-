Billing help

  • The Company Owner manages the subscription from Billing: update the payment method, download invoices, cancel or resume.
  • If a payment fails, choose Fix Payment on the Billing page to update your card. Your data stays safe while you do.
  • {{brand.ProductName}} costs {{brand.PricePerInterval}} per company, billed monthly. See the Subscription and Billing Policy, Cancellation Policy and Refund Policy.

Account access

  • Forgot your password? Use Forgot Password on the sign-in page to receive a reset link.
  • Didn’t receive an invitation? Ask your Company Owner or Administrator to invite you again from Company Admin.
  • Lost access to two-factor authentication? Ask your company administrator to contact support; we verify ownership before resetting access.
  • Manage your password, two-factor authentication and active sessions in Security settings.

Company administration

  • Invite team members, change roles and remove users in Company Admin. Team members are included in your subscription.
  • Roles: Company Owner (everything, including billing), Administrator (users and workspace configuration), Manager (manages records), Employee (day-to-day work).
  • Records can be exported from any list in your workspace with Export.

Security reporting

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability or that an account has been compromised, report it right away{{if brand.ContactEmail}} to {{brand.ContactEmail}} with the subject “Security report”{{end}}. Please include the steps to reproduce and do not access data that does not belong to you. We investigate every report and will keep you informed.
